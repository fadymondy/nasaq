Adds six CircleXO business-app brands to `@fadymondy/nasaq/brands` and the theme tokens: `matjar`, `sanduq`, `mizan`, `qaima`, `makhzan` and `mawared`. Each has a cube-lattice mark (copied verbatim from the products' marks table), a manifest with English and Arabic names and its circlexo.com site, and brand and action colours that pass contrast in light and dark.
