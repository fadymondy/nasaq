Add `PlacementSettings`: where a plugin or module appears in the app shell (sidebar, header, side panel, floating widget or hidden) as radio cards with a small shell diagram each, its order, and an optional "Open on start" switch. Keeps a draft, shows unsaved changes, saves only what changed, with Discard, saving and error states. Pure helpers `canBeDefaultPage`, `isModeAvailable`, `parseOrder`, `withMode`, `placementChanges`.
