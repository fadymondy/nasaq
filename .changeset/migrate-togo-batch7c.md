Add `PluginCard` and `PluginCardGrid`: a plugin with its icon and colour, name, version, kind, enabled state and last activity, description, a compact headline count with a sparkline, and Page / Details actions. `selectable` turns it into a checkbox card (click, checkbox or long press) for bulk actions. Pure helpers `pluginActivity`, `humanizeKind`, `toggleSelected`, `selectionState`.
