Usage

How the marks and colours are and are not used.

Do

  • Use the mark as supplied Take the file from this page. It is generated from the brand's specification.
  • Keep clear space Leave at least the width of one cube on every side.
  • Use the colour pairs listed The dark-ground colours are for dark grounds, the light ones for light.

Don't

  • Do not recolour the mark Its body and its accent cube keep their colours on every ground.
  • Do not mirror, stretch or redraw it It does not flip in right-to-left layouts.
  • Do not swap in a generic icon If the mark is unavailable, use the brand name as text.
  • Do not invent colours Use only the palette on this page.
